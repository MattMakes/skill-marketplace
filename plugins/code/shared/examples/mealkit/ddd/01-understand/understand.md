# Understand — Meal-kit subscription

## In plain words

We wrote down who this is for, what counts as working, and how big it is allowed to get.

**Decided:** One product: a weekly meal-kit box people subscribe to. Success is measured by weeks kept, not sign-ups.

**Assumed:** One person builds and runs the whole thing, so it must stay small enough for one head.

**Riskiest:** If a second team ever joins, almost every sizing call below was made for the wrong shape of team.
