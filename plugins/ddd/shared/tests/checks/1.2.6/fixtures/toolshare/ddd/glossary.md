# Ubiquitous language: Toolshare community tool-lending library

## Shared
**Member** - A resident household with a current membership; identified by a member number. _Avoid_: user, customer, borrower _(meaning differs by context - see below)_
**Membership** - A member's 12-month right to borrow, started when the annual fee is paid. _Avoid_: subscription, account
**Tool** - A kind of thing the library lends ('cordless drill'), with a replacement value and safety notes; in the desk and workshop: the physical item (see Item). _Avoid_: product, asset
**Item** - One asset-tagged physical unit of a tool, with a condition and a status (available, reserved, on loan, in maintenance, retired). _Avoid_: unit, copy, tool (at the desk) _(meaning differs by context - see below)_
**Reservation** - A 48-hour hold on an available item for one member. _Avoid_: booking, hold
**Loan** - One item in one member's hands from check-out to return, due in 14 days, renewable once by 7. _Avoid_: rental, hire, checkout
**Due date** - The day a loan must be returned by; the day after it the loan is overdue. _Avoid_: deadline, return date
**Late fee** - GBP 1 per item per overdue day, capped at the item's replacement value. _Avoid_: fine, penalty
**Balance** - What a member owes in late and damage fees; must be zero to borrow. _Avoid_: debt, outstanding
**Inspection** - A maintenance volunteer's safety and condition check of an item; pass or fail. _Avoid_: check, audit

## Membership context
**Member** — a person with contact details, a member number, a rate (standard or concession) and a term that expires. _Avoid_: user, customer, borrower _(differs from the shared meaning — a boundary)_
_Uses the shared meaning of_: **Membership**

## Inventory context
**Tool** — a catalogue kind with replacement value, safety notes and induction flag; owns its items. _Avoid_: product, asset _(differs from the shared meaning — a boundary)_
**Item** — one asset-tagged physical unit with a condition and a lifecycle (fit, in maintenance, retired). _Avoid_: unit, copy, tool (at the desk) _(differs from the shared meaning — a boundary)_
_Uses the shared meaning of_: **Inspection**

## Lending context
**Item** — a lendable unit with an availability (available, reserved, on loan); condition is not modelled here. _(differs from the shared meaning — a boundary)_
**Member** — a borrower identified by member number with a standing (may borrow or not) - no contact details. _(differs from the shared meaning — a boundary)_
_Uses the shared meaning of_: **Reservation**, **Loan**, **Due date**

## Fees context
**Member** — an account holder with a balance in pence; nothing else about the person. _(differs from the shared meaning — a boundary)_
**Fee account** — A member's running ledger of late fees, damage charges, payments and waivers; its balance is what the member owes.
_Uses the shared meaning of_: **Late fee**, **Balance**
