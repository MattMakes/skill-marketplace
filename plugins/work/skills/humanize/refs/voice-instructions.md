# Voice Instructions

<!-- Adapted from blader/humanizer (MIT, Siqi Chen); see ../LICENSE-humanizer. -->

Scrubbing tells is half the job. Sterile, voiceless prose is as recognisable as slop. Good writing
has a person behind it. Apply this after the scrub, before the audit, and only where the register
allows: a release note or a personal post wants a pulse; an API reference does not.

## Signs of soulless writing, even when technically clean

- Every sentence has the same length and shape
- Neutral reporting, no opinions
- No acknowledgement of uncertainty or mixed feelings
- No first person where first person would be honest
- No humour, no edge, no personality
- Reads like an encyclopaedia entry or a press release

## How to add voice

**Have opinions.** React to facts instead of listing them. "I genuinely don't know how to feel about this" is more human than a neutral pros-and-cons table.

**Vary the rhythm.** Short sentences. Then longer ones that take their time getting where they are going.

**Acknowledge complexity.** People have mixed feelings. "Impressive, and a little unsettling" beats "impressive".

**Use "I" when it fits.** First person is not unprofessional. "I keep coming back to..." signals a real person thinking.

**Let some mess in.** Perfect structure feels algorithmic. A tangent or an aside is human.

**Be specific about feelings.** Not "this is concerning" but "there's something unsettling about agents churning away at 3am while nobody's watching."

## Before, clean but soulless

> The experiment produced interesting results. The agents generated 3 million lines of code. Some developers were impressed while others were skeptical. The implications remain unclear.

## After, has a pulse

> I genuinely don't know how to feel about this one. 3 million lines of code, generated while the humans presumably slept. Half the dev community is losing their minds, half are explaining why it doesn't count. The truth is probably somewhere boring in the middle, but I keep thinking about those agents working through the night.
