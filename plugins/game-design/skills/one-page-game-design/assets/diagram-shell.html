Beaconfall Courier

Scope: the repeatable journey from accepting a delivery to improving the lighthouse network.

Revision: 0.3

Date: 2026-09-27

Audience: design team and playtesters

Journey and resource loop

A design diagram with five stages. The courier accepts a contract, plans a route, crosses a storm, delivers a beacon part, and upgrades the boat. Supplies support crossing the storm. Trust earned from delivery unlocks harder contracts.

Player experience

Make tense, informed commitments and see isolated islands reconnect because of each delivery.

Key rules

  • Accept only 1 active contract.
  • Choose a route before the weather is revealed.
  • A failed crossing costs time or cargo, never the full campaign.

Feedback and consequences

  • Forecasts show likely wave patterns.
  • Boat damage changes steering immediately.
  • Lit beacons make later routes safer.

Unresolved choices

  • Does late delivery reduce trust or change the story?
  • Can players abandon cargo to save fuel?
  • Playtest: can a new player predict storm risk after 1 voyage?
