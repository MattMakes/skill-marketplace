# Deck: scopes

How much one solo developer can build. Draw one. The scope limits content, not quality.

1. game jam: 48 hours, one mechanic, one screen
2. weekend prototype: 3 days, the core loop only
3. one month solo: one polished loop and about 10 levels or 30 minutes of content
4. three months solo: a small complete game, about 2 hours of content
5. six months solo: a commercial-scale small game, about 5 hours of content
