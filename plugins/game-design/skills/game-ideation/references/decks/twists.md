# Deck: twists

The one rule that makes the concept surprising. Draw one. Every entry is boundary-safe. When you
add an entry, keep it that way.

1. time loops every few minutes and you keep only what you learned
2. gravity changes direction on a timer
3. you are very small
4. the world reacts only to sound
5. the whole game fits on one screen
6. you cooperate with a recording of your previous attempt
7. the game has only one button
8. the map rewrites itself when you are not looking
9. every character remembers what you did yesterday
10. the weather is the main obstacle
11. you cannot see and navigate by sound alone
12. colors are a resource you spend
13. things age in real time between sessions
14. the level is a living, friendly creature
15. you play the environment, not a character
16. one rule of the game changes each day
17. you can move only while the music plays
18. every object can talk and has an opinion
19. resources are shared with other players who are never online at the same time
20. you win by teaching someone else to do it
21. the goal is to leave no trace
22. you undo things instead of doing them
23. the camera is a character with its own wishes
24. a season passes every minute
25. every door is one-way
26. everything is made of paper and can fold
27. you swap roles with the character you are helping
28. your inventory holds exactly one item
29. light is the only currency
30. the world has a mirror twin that copies you with a delay
31. messages from past players wash up on the shore
32. the rules are written on the objects themselves
